Project Insights

Frequently
Asked Questions

I

What is textile design?

Textile design involves creating patterns, textures, and surface designs for fabrics. It combines creativity, colour theory, and material understanding to develop visually appealing textile concepts.

II

What was the purpose of these textile design projects?

These projects were developed to explore pattern creation, colour relationships, and surface design techniques while building a deeper understanding of textile applications within fashion.

III

What skills were demonstrated in this work?

The work demonstrates skills in pattern development, colour application, textile experimentation, visual composition, and creative surface design.

IV

What challenges were encountered during the process?

Creating balanced repeat patterns, selecting effective colour combinations, and maintaining consistency across textile designs required experimentation and refinement.
