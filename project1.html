The work focuses on communicating design concepts through expressive illustrations that capture silhouette, movement, proportion, and aesthetic identity.

Project Insights

Frequently
Asked Questions

I

What is draping in fashion design?

Draping is a design technique where fabric is arranged directly on a dress form to explore shape, volume, and garment structure before creating flat patterns.

II

What was the purpose of these draping assignments?

These assignments were developed to deepen understanding of fabric behaviour, silhouette development, and three-dimensional garment construction through practical experimentation.

III

What skills were demonstrated in this work?

The projects showcase skills in fabric manipulation, creative draping techniques, design development, visual balance, and considered garment presentation.

IV

What challenges were encountered during the process?

Working with different fabric weights and achieving balanced proportions required continuous experimentation and refinement throughout the design process.
