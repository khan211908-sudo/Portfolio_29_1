Project Insights

Frequently
Asked Questions

I

What is digital fashion?

Digital fashion combines fashion design with technology, enabling designers to create garments, visualisations, and concepts within digital environments before physical production.

II

What was the purpose of these projects?

These projects were developed to explore digital design workflows, strengthen technical software skills, and understand how technology is transforming the fashion industry.

III

What skills were demonstrated in this work?

The work demonstrates skills in digital illustration, fashion visualisation, creative concept development, software proficiency, and digital presentation techniques.

IV

What challenges were encountered during the process?

Learning new digital tools, refining virtual design details, and balancing creativity with technical accuracy required continuous experimentation and problem-solving.
